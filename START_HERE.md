## 💡 Pro Tips for Beginners

### Do These Things:
✅ **Start small** - Make Pong before trying to make Call of Duty
✅ **Follow tutorials** - Complete at least 2-3 full tutorials
✅ **Type the code yourself** - Don't just copy-paste, understand it
✅ **Break things** - Experiment, see what happens
✅ **Save often** - Press Ctrl+S frequently
✅ **Ask questions** - The Godot community is super helpful
✅ **Celebrate small wins** - Moved a sprite? Awesome! That's progress!

### Don't Do These:
❌ Don't start with your "dream game"
❌ Don't skip the basics
❌ Don't get discouraged by complexity
❌ Don't compare your beginning to someone's middle
❌ Don't ignore errors (read them, they help!)
❌ Don't give up after one difficult moment
